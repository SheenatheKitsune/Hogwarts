Dette er en liste over alle mine endpoints, som jeg har anvendt! Har kategoriseret dem ud fra de forskellige filer, hvad der er anvendt i disse filer, for at få et vist bestemt data, fra databasen!

Houses endpoints:
GET /houses || Get all houses
GET /houses/:id || Get single house - For specifikt ønskede id!!!

Teachers endpoints:
GET /teachers || Get all teachers
GET /teachers/:id || Get single teacher - For specifikt ønskede id!!!
POST /teachers || POST new teacher! - For at add en ny teacher til databasen!!!
PUT /teachers/:id || PUT single teacher - Opdaterer specifikt teacher ud fra ønskede id!!!
DEL /teachers/:id || DELETE single teahcer - Fjerner specifikt teacher ud fra ønsket id!!!

Students endpoints:
GET /students || Get all students
GET /students/:id || Get single student - For specifikt ønskede id!!!
POST /students || POST new student! - For at add en ny student til databasen!!!
PUT /students/:id || PUT single student - Opdaterer specifikt student ud fra ønskede id!!!
DEL /students/:id || DELETE single student - Fjerner specifikt student ud fra ønsket id!!!

Courses endpoints:
GET /courses || Get all courses
GET /courses/:id || Get single course - for specifikt ønskede id!!!
